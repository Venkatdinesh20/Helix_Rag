Northbeam Forecast — Product FAQ (Fictional demo document)

Northbeam Forecast is a fictional product used to demonstrate the Helix RAG system.

What is Northbeam Forecast?
Northbeam Forecast predicts weekly product demand for retailers with 10 to 500 stores. It combines point-of-sale history, promotions, holidays and local weather to forecast demand up to 26 weeks ahead.

How accurate are the forecasts?
Across pilot customers, the median weekly forecast error (WMAPE) is 18% at store-and-product level and 7% at regional level. Accuracy is usually lower for new products with less than 8 weeks of sales history.

What data do customers need to provide?
At minimum, two years of daily point-of-sale data by store and product, a product catalogue, and a promotions calendar. Inventory levels and weather feeds are optional but improve accuracy.

How often are forecasts updated?
Forecasts refresh every night at 02:00 local time. Customers on the Enterprise plan can trigger an extra on-demand refresh up to 4 times per day.

Which plans are available?
- Starter: up to 25 stores, weekly forecasts, email support.
- Growth: up to 150 stores, daily forecasts, promotions modelling, chat support.
- Enterprise: unlimited stores, on-demand refreshes, a dedicated customer success manager, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Where is customer data stored?
All customer data is stored in Canadian data centres and encrypted at rest and in transit. Northbeam does not use one customer's data to train models for another customer.

Can Forecast integrate with other systems?
Yes. Northbeam provides connectors for common retail systems, an export to CSV and Parquet, and a REST API. API keys are created by customer administrators and expire after 12 months.

How long does onboarding take?
A typical onboarding takes 6 to 8 weeks: 2 weeks for data connection, 3 weeks for model training and back-testing, and 1 to 3 weeks for user training and go-live.

What happens if forecasts look wrong?
Users can flag a forecast in the app. Flags are reviewed by the data science team within 2 working days, and recurring issues are added to the monthly model review.
